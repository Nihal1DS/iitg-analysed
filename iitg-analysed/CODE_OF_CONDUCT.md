# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of IITG Analysed pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, discipline, gender, age, or experience level.

## Our Standards

**Positive behaviour includes:**
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the IITG community

**Unacceptable behaviour includes:**
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional or academic setting

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to [nihalpatel.2802@gmail.com](mailto:nihalpatel.2802@gmail.com). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
